1. Welcome to GRITGO

GRITGO is not just a habit tracker — it's your complete personal growth operating system. It combines habits, health, sleep, workouts, journaling, AI coaching, digital library, and photo progress into one powerful app.

This guide covers every single feature you'll find inside the app.

💡 Pro Tip — Install as App

Open this in your mobile browser and click "Add to Home Screen". It will work exactly like a native app (no browser bar, works offline).

⭐ What Makes GRITGO Different

Most habit apps only track checkboxes. GRITGO tracks 50+ data points across every area of your life and uses AI to analyze them all together — so you get real coaching, not generic motivation.

2. Challenge Setup (First Time)

When you first open GRITGO, you'll see a welcome screen. You need to set up your first challenge:

  • Challenge Name: e.g., "90-Day Discipline", "Morning Routine", "Weight Loss Journey"
  • Duration: Number of days (e.g., 30, 60, 90)
  • Start Date: Can't be in the past — must be today or future
  • Motivation Text: A sentence that will appear on your dashboard
  • Day Start / Day End Time: When your "day" starts and ends (e.g., 05:00 → 21:00). This affects when the new day begins for tracking.

Click Start Challenge to begin.

⚠️ Start Date Lock

You can change the start date later in Settings, but you cannot set a fresh start date that's in the past.

4. Daily Habits (The Core)

➕ Adding a Habit

  • Tap + Add Habit
  • Type the habit name (e.g., "Drink 2L Water")
  • Choose a Category (General, Study, Health, Fitness, Work, Mindfulness, Social, Finance, Hobby, Sleep, Morning Routine)
  • Pick an Icon from 9 options (book, fitness, water, run, mind, sleep, heart, star, default)
  • Tap +Add Habit

✏️ Editing / Reordering / Deleting

  • Tap Edit habits to enter edit mode
  • ↑ ↓ arrows move a habit up or down
  • Tap the ✏️ pencil icon to rename / change category
  • Tap the 🗑️ trash icon to delete — you'll get a 5-second UNDO option
  • Tap Back to discard changes or Done editing to save

✅ Checking Habits

  • Tap the numbered box (1, 2, 3...) next to a habit → it turns into a checkmark ✓
  • You get a satisfying sound + haptic vibration
  • Tap again to uncheck
  • When you complete ALL habits → confetti + celebration sound + AI congratulation message

🔍 Search Bar

Type a habit name or number (e.g., "3") to filter instantly.

👁️ Hide Completed

Tap the eye icon next to the search bar to hide completed habits. A slash-through eye means completed habits are hidden. Persists across sessions.

🔗 Chain Lock (Auto)

Habits are chained — a habit gets dimmed (opacity 0.4) until the previous habit is done. This builds momentum. When the previous one is done, the next one glows with a cyan border — inviting you to do it.

⚡ Quick Action Buttons

  • ✅ Check All (blue button) — marks every habit as done
  • 🔄 Reset All (black button) — unchecks every habit

💡 Pro Tip — Timer Icon

Each habit has a small ⏱ clock icon next to it — use it to start a focus timer. Read section 6.

5. Heatmap & Progress

📅 Dashboard Heatmap

Below the stats, you'll see a grid of small boxes — one for each day of your challenge.

  • Dark box = 0% completed
  • Dim cyan = 33-65% completed
  • Bright cyan = 66-99% completed
  • Full glow (cyan with shadow) = 100% perfect day
  • White border = Today

🖱️ Tap to Edit Past Days (Past Edit)

  • Tap any past day in the heatmap → a popup confirms you want to edit
  • Tap Tick to open that day's habit list
  • You can check/uncheck habits for that past date
  • Has its own search bar too

⚠️ Can't Edit Future Days

You can only edit today or past days. Future days are locked.

📊 The 3 Stat Cards (Top of Home)

  • 🔥 Day Streak — consecutive days of 100% completion
  • 🏆 Perfect Days — total days where you completed 100%
  • 📈 Overall % — today's completion percentage

📆 Yearly Heatmap (GitHub Style)

Go to Extras & Insights → scroll down → Yearly Heatmap & Photos → Open Yearly View. You'll see a full-year, month-by-month grid view of every day.

6. Advanced Timer

Every habit has a small ⏱ clock icon. Tap it to open the timer modal.

  • Presets: 5 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 3 hours, 5 hours
  • Custom: Type any time (e.g., "45" for 45 minutes, or "1.5h" for 90 minutes)
  • A floating timer widget appears on your screen showing the countdown
  • When the timer ends → alarm sound plays + the habit is automatically checked off + confetti!
  • Tap Stop Timer on the widget to cancel anytime

💡 Pro Tip

Use the timer for tasks like "Read for 30 minutes" or "Meditate for 10 minutes". You don't need to manually check it afterward — the timer does it for you.

23. FAQ & Pro Tips

❓ What if I miss a day?

Tap the past day in the heatmap → Past Edit → check the habits you actually did. Streak recalculates.

❓ Why is the AI Voice not working?

Grant microphone permission to the browser. Also ensure internet is on. Some browsers (Chrome) work better than others.

❓ How does the Streak work?

Streak = consecutive days of 100% completion. Miss one day → streak resets to 0. But your Perfect Days count stays.

❓ Why is the AI not adding habits when I ask?

Phrase it clearly: "Add habit: Drink 2L Water". The AI returns a JSON array that the app catches. If it doesn't work, try "Add these habits: X, Y, Z".

❓ Can I use multiple challenges?

No — one active challenge at a time. But you can change the challenge name/duration in Settings, or Backup → Reset → new challenge (then restore old backup later if needed).

❓ What happens if I clear browser cache?

All local data is lost. Restore from your backup text you saved earlier.

❓ Does the AI remember previous conversations?

Yes — it keeps the last 10 messages as context. But clearing chat wipes this memory.

❓ Why is a habit dimmed and uncheckable?

That's the Chain Lock — the previous habit isn't done yet. Do it in order or tap the previous one first.

❓ Can I edit a past message in the chat?

Yes — long-press → Edit → the message returns to the input, edit it, then send. The message and everything after it is deleted before re-sending.

❓ What's the "speedometer hold" thing?

In Extras & Insights, hold your finger on the gauge → the needle revs + engine sound + vibration. It's a fun Easter egg! 🏎️

❓ How do I add book rules to tracking?

Library → open any book → Ask AI → tell AI "Add these rules to my tracker" → they appear in Book Rules Tracker in the sidebar.

❓ Can I export my data to Excel?

Yes — Settings → Export Data as CSV. Copy → paste into Excel or Google Sheets.
