Welcome to GRITGO

GRITGO is not just an app; it's your ultimate tool for building discipline. This guide will walk you through every feature, from the smallest details to the advanced AI systems.

💡 Pro Tip

To get the best experience, open this site in your mobile browser and click "Add to Home Screen" to install it like a native app.

Daily Habits & Heatmap

This is the core of the app. Here you will track your daily tasks and build your streak.

1. Adding & Editing Habits

  • Click the + Add Habit button to create a new habit.
  • You can select the habit's name, category (e.g., Study, Health), and icon.
  • Click Edit habits to rename, reorder, or delete existing habits.

2. Checking Off Habits

Tap the numbered box (e.g., 1, 2, 3) next to a habit to mark it done. You will get a satisfying sound and haptic vibration. If you accidentally check it, just tap it again to uncheck.

3. The Heatmap

The grid of small boxes represents your entire challenge.

  • The more habits you complete in a day, the brighter the box becomes (Cyan color).
  • Clicking on a specific day's box lets you open a popup to view or edit that day (Past Edit).

Advanced Timer

To help you focus, there is a clock (⏱️) icon next to each habit.

  • Clicking the clock icon opens the timer settings.
  • You can set preset times (5m, 30m, 1h, 3h) or enter a custom time.
  • When the timer ends, an alarm sounds and the habit is automatically checked off.

Smart AI Coach

Your personal AI coach. It analyzes your data and provides motivation whenever you need it.

Key Features:

  • 🎤 Voice Input: Speak in any language. The AI will understand and reply in the same language.
  • 📷 Send Photos: Send images (like a meal or gym photo) to the AI for analysis.
  • AI Persona: Go to Settings to change the AI's personality (Strict, Zen, Funny, Normal).
  • Chat Controls: Swipe right on any message to reply. Long-press a message to copy, edit, or delete it.

🤖 AI Habit Generator

If you tell the AI "Give me 5 healthy habits to lose weight", it will intelligently generate them and add them directly to your habit list!

Health Trackers (Water & Sleep)

💧 Daily Water Tracker (HydroFlow)

Go to "Daily Water Tracker" to monitor your hydration.

  • Use the +250ml Glass and +500ml Bottle buttons to log water.
  • When you reach your daily goal, you'll get a celebration animation and a confetti blast.

😴 Sleep Tracker

Open "Weekly Progress & Sleep Tracker" to log your sleep.

  • Select how many hours you slept.
  • The AI will analyze your "Brain State" (Tired, Fresh, Jammed) and give you personalized feedback.

Quit Habit Tracker

This section is designed to help you break bad habits (e.g., smoking, junk food).

  • Go to Extras & Insights and open the Quit Tracker.
  • Add your bad habit. It will track your Clean Streak in days, hours, and minutes.
  • 🚨 I Need Help Button: If you have a sudden craving, tap this button. The AI will instantly step in as an emergency coach to talk you out of it.

Workout Tracker

Track your gym or home workouts with precision.

  • Log the Sets, Reps, and Weight for each exercise.
  • Check off completed sets to automatically start a 90-second Rest Timer.
  • Your heaviest lifted weight is automatically saved as a PR (Personal Record).
  • Track your total volume and workout history over time.

Extras & Insights

Your analytics dashboard. Here you can see the big picture of your progress.

  • Daily Motivation: Get a new motivational quote every day.
  • 30-Day Habit Trends: View beautiful charts to see which habits are improving and which need work.
  • Report Card: Generate a shareable image of your stats to post on Instagram or send to friends.
  • AI Master Time Planner: Tell the AI your goal, and it will build a time-blocked daily routine for you.

Settings & Backup

Customize your app and keep your data safe.

  • App Themes: Choose from over 50+ themes to completely change the look of the app.
  • Data Backup: To save your progress, click Backup Data and copy the text to a safe place.
  • CSV Export: Export your habit data to an Excel/Google Sheets file.

⚠️ Important Warning

Clearing your browser cache or data will delete your app progress. Always keep a backup text saved in your notes app once a week.

FAQ & Pro Tips

Q: What if I miss a day?

A: Don't stress! Go to the Heatmap, click on the past day, and use "Past Edit" to check off any habits you completed but forgot to log.

Q: Why is the AI Voice not working?

A: Make sure you have granted microphone permissions to your browser. You also need an active internet connection for the AI to process your speech.

Q: How does the Streak work?

A: Your streak increases for every consecutive day you complete 100% of your habits. If you miss a single day, the streak resets to 0 (but your Perfect Days count stays safe).
